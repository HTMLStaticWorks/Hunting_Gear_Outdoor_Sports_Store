THE STORY OF VANGUARD OUTPOST

How a shattered riflescope mount during a 1998 Gallatin blizzard ignited a quarter-century quest for indestructible backcountry hardware.

28 YEARS OF RUGGED FIELD INTEGRITY

FORGED IN THE FROZEN SHADOWS OF THE ROCKIES

In November 1998, high on the scree ridges of Montana’s Madison Range, founder and outfitter Cole Dalton had a trophy bull elk in his crosshairs. When his horse slipped on sheer black ice, the rifle scabbard struck frozen shale. The commercial optic mount sheared cleanly at the screws.

That failed hunt became a catalyst. Returning to Bozeman, Dalton retreated into an unheated machine shop, milling monolithic scope rings from solid blocks of 7075-T6 aircraft aluminum. Friends took notice. Guides demanded sets. Within two seasons, the Vanguard Outpost armory was born.

1998 • BOZEMAN GARAGE FOUNDATION

The First Monolithic Billet Ring

Precision CNC milled from single billet blocks to eliminate structural weak points. Zero mechanical screws failed during severe 50-caliber recoil testing.

2012 • THE CRYO-SPEC REVOLUTION

Extreme Cold-Chamber Certification

Established our internal -40°F cryo testing laboratory and pneumatic 1,200G impact shock chambers. Every lens seal and turret lock became argon-purged for life.

2026 • GLOBAL EXPEDITION VANGUARD

Equipping Outfitters Across 6 Continents

Now supplying elite mountain hunters, search-and-rescue teams, and wildlife conservationists worldwide with optics, apparel, and load-hauling frames.
