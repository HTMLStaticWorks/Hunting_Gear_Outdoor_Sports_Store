HUNTER CONSERVATION

THE FAIR CHASE CODE & WILDLANDS STEWARDSHIP

Hunting is conservation. We reject canned hunts, digital crosshair assistance, and unfair pursuit technology.

FOUNDATION STANDARD BOONE & CROCKETT ETHICS

THE 4 TENETS OF FAIR CHASE

  • 1. Equal Opportunity Escape: The animal must possess free natural avenues of escape in unfenced territory.
  • 2. No Motorized Pursuit: Zero motorized conveyance or airborne assistance in locating or stalking quarry.
  • 3. Complete Harvest Utilization: All edible game meat is conscientiously salvaged, processed, and respected.
  • 4. Habitat Reinvestment: 3% of all Vanguard outfitter proceeds go directly to Rocky Mountain Elk & BHA public access trusts.
