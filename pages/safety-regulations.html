LEGAL SHOOTING HOURS

Strictly enforced: exactly one-half hour before civil sunrise to one-half hour after civil sunset. Consult precise GPS-calibrated solar tables for your specific hunting unit.

CARCASS VALIDATION

TAGGING & EVIDENCE OF SEX

You must immediately notch and attach the carcass tag prior to moving or dressing the animal. Evidence of sex (naturally attached reproductive organs or head with antlers) must remain attached.

Immediate Notching Evidence Attached CWD Mandatory Check
CONVEYANCE CODES

VEHICLE & ATV TRANSPORT

All firearms must be completely unloaded in both chamber and magazine while inside or mounted on any motorized vehicle, ATV, or snowmobile. Bows must be un-nocked and encased.

Unloaded Chamber Hard-Sided Lock No Road Hunting
UNIVERSAL PROTOCOLS

THE 4 COMMANDMENTS OF BACKCOUNTRY SAFETY

Drill these rules until they become involuntary reflexes. Negligence in the wild cannot be undone.

COMMANDMENT 01

MUZZLE CONTROL

Control the direction of your firearm muzzle at all times. Never let the muzzle sweep across a hunting partner, canine, or vehicle.

Zero Accidental Pointing Mechanical Safety On
COMMANDMENT 02

TARGET IDENTIFICATION

Positively identify your quarry with binoculars before raising your weapon. Verify what lies in front of and hundreds of yards behind your target.

Never Scope Humans Identify Backstop
COMMANDMENT 03

TREESTAND HARNESS

Wear a full-body Fall Arrest System (FAS) harness attached to the tree from the second your boots leave the ground until they touch down again.

Lineman's Rope Suspension Relief Strap
COMMANDMENT 04

BEAR DEFENSE TRIAGE

In Grizzly country, keep EPA-registered bear spray holstered on your hip — not packed away in your bag. Hang all game meat 100 yards downwind, 15 feet high.

Bear Spray on Hip 100-Yard Meat Cache
