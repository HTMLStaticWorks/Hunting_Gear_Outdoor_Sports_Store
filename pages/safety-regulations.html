BLAZE ORANGE LAWS

HIGH-VISIBILITY GARMENT COMPLIANCE

During modern firearm big game seasons, most western and midwestern states require a minimum of 400 to 500 square inches of solid fluorescent hunter orange worn above the waist, visible from 360 degrees.

  • • Montana: 400 sq. inches of hunter orange hat/vest required for big game.
  • • Wyoming: Solid hunter orange hat or vest mandatory during rifle season.
  • • Idaho: Mandatory for youth hunters; highly advised state-wide.
  • • Camo Orange: Only permitted in select states (e.g. Colorado requires solid).

E-TAGGING PROTOCOL

TAG NOTCHING & CARCASS TRANSPORT

Immediate physical tag notching or electronic app validation (e.g., MyFWP, WDFW e-tag) is mandatory before moving any harvested big game animal from the kill site.

  • • Immediate Notching: Month and date must be cleanly punched immediately upon kill.
  • • Chronic Wasting Disease (CWD): Interstate transport of brain/spinal tissue is prohibited.
  • • Proof of Sex: Head or evidence of sex must remain naturally attached to carcass.
  • • Check Station: All hunters must stop at state highway check stations when open.

UNIVERSAL PROTOCOLS

THE 4 COMMANDMENTS OF BACKCOUNTRY SAFETY

Drill these rules until they become involuntary reflexes. Negligence in the wild cannot be undone.

COMMANDMENT 01

MUZZLE CONTROL

Control the direction of your firearm muzzle at all times. Never let the muzzle sweep across a hunting partner, canine, or vehicle.

Zero Accidental Pointing Mechanical Safety On
COMMANDMENT 02

TARGET IDENTIFICATION

Positively identify your quarry with binoculars before raising your weapon. Verify what lies in front of and hundreds of yards behind your target.

Never Scope Humans Identify Backstop
COMMANDMENT 03

TREESTAND HARNESS

Wear a full-body Fall Arrest System (FAS) harness attached to the tree from the second your boots leave the ground until they touch down again.

Lineman's Rope Suspension Relief Strap
COMMANDMENT 04

BEAR DEFENSE TRIAGE

In Grizzly country, keep EPA-registered bear spray holstered on your hip — not packed away in your bag. Hang all game meat 100 yards downwind, 15 feet high.
